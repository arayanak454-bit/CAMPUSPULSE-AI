# 🎓 CampusPulse — AI-Powered Campus Operating System

CampusPulse AI

An AI-Powered Campus Operating System for Safety, Grievance, and Student Life

CampusPulse is a unified digital platform designed to make large college campuses safer, more responsive, and better connected.

Instead of asking:

«"How can we build another college management app?"»

CampusPulse starts with a different question:

«"What if a campus could understand problems as they happen, route them to the right people, and continuously improve from what students report?"»

The idea is built around a simple principle:

REPORT → UNDERSTAND → RESPOND → RESOLVE → VERIFY → IMPROVE

---

The Problem

Modern college campuses can function like small cities.

Students may need to deal with:

- Safety concerns
- Harassment and grievances
- Lost and found items
- Traffic and reckless driving
- Parking problems
- Hostel or infrastructure issues
- Academic concerns
- Faculty-related queries
- Emergency situations
- Lack of communication between students and administration

The problem is not always the absence of individual systems.

The bigger problem is that these systems are often fragmented.

A student may report an issue through one channel, contact security through another, search for information somewhere else, and have no clear way to know what happened after submitting a complaint.

This creates a gap between:

A problem being reported

and

A problem actually being resolved.

---

The Core Idea

CampusPulse treats the campus as a connected ecosystem rather than a collection of separate departments.

The platform brings multiple student-facing workflows into one system:

- Safety & Grievance
- Complaint Tracking
- Campus AI Assistant
- Lost & Found
- Traffic & Parking
- Student Planner
- Community Updates
- Faculty Hub
- Administrative Analytics

The objective is not to replace existing college systems.

Instead, CampusPulse acts as an intelligent campus operating layer that connects important student and administrative workflows.

---

Problem-First Thinking

CampusPulse does not begin with:

«"We have AI. Where can we use it?"»

It begins with:

«"Students face problems, but reporting, response and follow-up are often disconnected. How can technology reduce that gap?"»

AI and automation are then used where they provide practical value.

For example:

A student reports a problem.

↓

AI understands and categorizes the report.

↓

The system identifies its priority and relevant department.

↓

The complaint is assigned.

↓

The responsible authority updates its status.

↓

The student can track progress.

↓

The issue is resolved.

↓

The student verifies the resolution.

This creates a measurable feedback loop.

Safety & Grievance

Safety is one of the central use cases of CampusPulse.

Students can report concerns and track the progress of their complaints through a structured workflow.

Possible categories include:

- Harassment
- Unsafe locations
- Suspicious activity
- Ragging
- Bullying
- Physical violence
- Infrastructure-related safety issues
- Other campus grievances

The platform is designed for all students, while allowing specialised workflows for sensitive cases such as women's safety.

CampusPulse does not claim that technology can prevent every crime.

Instead, it focuses on:

Safe Reporting → Faster Routing → Accountability → Escalation → Verification

---

AI Layer

AI is not used simply as a chatbot.

It can support the campus ecosystem through:

Complaint Understanding

Understand the student's description and identify the likely category.

Priority Assistance

Identify potentially urgent or high-risk reports for faster attention.

Smart Routing

Suggest the appropriate department or responsible authority.

Summarization

Convert long descriptions into concise summaries for administrators.

Campus Assistant

Help students find information, services and relevant campus resources.

Pattern Detection

Identify recurring issues, hotspots and trends from aggregated campus data.

Important Principle

AI should remain a decision-support system, especially for sensitive cases.

Final high-impact decisions should remain with authorized human personnel.

Privacy & Security

Campus safety platforms deal with sensitive information.

Therefore, CampusPulse is designed around principles such as:

- Role-based access
- Authentication
- Minimal data exposure
- Secure APIs
- Encryption in production
- Audit logs
- Controlled access to sensitive reports
- Human verification for high-impact decisions

Sensitive information should only be available to authorized personnel.

---

Preventing Misuse

A system dealing with complaints and safety must also prevent abuse.

Potential safeguards include:

- Verification workflows
- Moderation
- Abuse reporting
- Rate limiting
- Audit trails
- Role-based permissions
- Human review
- Escalation mechanisms

AI should not independently determine guilt or take irreversible action against a student.

Safeguards Against Unhealthy Gamification

CampusPulse should avoid mechanics that encourage excessive or unhealthy engagement.

Therefore:

- No endless engagement loops
- No pressure to maintain unrealistic streaks
- No public ranking of sensitive complaints
- No rewards for reporting serious incidents
- No competition around safety incidents
- No manipulation of students through constant notifications
- No gamification of victim experiences

The goal is to encourage healthy participation, not screen-time.

## 🚀 Dev Server Quickstart (0.0.0.0 Multi-Device Support)

CampusPulse runs on a multi-device development server listening on `0.0.0.0:8080` (all network interfaces). This allows access from both the development PC and any mobile phone/tablet on the same Wi-Fi.

### 🌐 Access URLs:
- **Computer URL:** [**http://localhost:8080/**](http://localhost:8080)
- **Phone / Wi-Fi URL:** [**http://10.10.186.31:8080/**](http://10.10.186.31:8080/)
  *(Connect your Android / iOS phone to the same Wi-Fi network and open `http://10.10.186.31:8080` in Chrome/Safari).*

---

### 💻 Starting the Server:
Run this command from the project directory:
```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "server.ps1"
```
Or simply double-click:
```bat
start_chrome.bat
```

---

### 🛡️ Windows Firewall Note (If Phone Cannot Connect):
If your Android phone fails to connect, Windows Defender Firewall may be blocking inbound traffic on port `8080`.
To allow access, run this command in **PowerShell as Administrator**:
```powershell
New-NetFirewallRule -DisplayName "CampusPulse Dev Server Port 8080" -Direction Inbound -LocalPort 8080 -Protocol TCP -Action Allow
```
Or allow PowerShell when prompted by the Windows Security alert on Private networks.
